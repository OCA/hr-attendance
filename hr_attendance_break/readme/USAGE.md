To record a break:

1. Check in from the attendance menu in the top bar (the attendance circle).
2. While checked in, click **Start Break** in the same menu. The button turns
   into **End Break**.
3. Click **End Break** when the break is over. The break is stored with its start
   and end time.
4. Repeat as many times as needed during the day.

When the employee checks out, any break that is still running is automatically
closed.

On the **attendance kiosk**, a checked-in employee who selects their name (and
enters their PIN if required) is offered a choice between *Start/End Break* and
*Check out*, so breaks can be recorded from a shared terminal too.

To review breaks:

- Open an attendance record to see its breaks and the resulting *Break Hours* and
  *Net Worked Hours*.
- Go to *Attendances > Reporting > Breaks* for a list and pivot view of all
  breaks, grouped by employee, day or break type.
