To record a break:

1. Check in as usual, from the attendance menu in the top bar or the kiosk.
2. To start a break, check out and pick a break reason (for example *Lunch
   Break*).
3. To end the break, check in again. The break is closed and the attendance of
   the day is reopened, instead of a new attendance being created.
4. Repeat as many times as needed during the day, and check out without a break
   reason at the end of the day.

A break that is not ended on the same day (the employee went home after
starting a break) is discarded: the attendance keeps the check out time of the
break start, and the next check in starts a new attendance.

To review breaks:

- Open an attendance record to see its breaks and the resulting *Break Hours*,
  *Unpaid Break Hours* and *Net Worked Hours*.
- Go to *Attendances > Reporting > Breaks* for a list and pivot view of all
  breaks, grouped by employee, day or break type, with paid / unpaid filters.
