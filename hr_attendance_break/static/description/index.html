HR Attendance Break

This module records employee breaks during their attendance, for labour-compliance reporting (are breaks taken, are they too long?) and to compute the worked hours net of unpaid breaks.

Breaks are recorded with the usual check out / check in buttons, using the attendance reasons of hr_attendance_reason: checking out with a reason linked to a break type starts a break, and the next check in ends it. The attendance of the day is reopened, so a working day stays one attendance that can hold several breaks (morning coffee, lunch, afternoon coffee).

Each break type is either paid or unpaid. For every attendance the module computes:

  • Break Hours: the total time spent on breaks.
  • Unpaid Break Hours: the unpaid break time. Time overlapping the lunch break of the employee’s working schedule is not counted, as Odoo already excludes it from the worked hours.
  • Net Worked Hours: the worked hours minus the unpaid breaks.

The worked hours computed by Odoo are left untouched.

Configuration

To configure breaks:

  1. Go to Attendances > Configuration > Break Types and review the break types. Paid Break and Unpaid Break are created on installation.
  2. Go to Attendances > Configuration > Reasons and create a reason for each kind of break (for example Coffee Break and Lunch Break), with Action Type set to Sign out, Show on attendance screen? checked and the matching Break Type.
  3. Go to Attendances > Configuration > Settings and, under Attendance Reasons, enable showing the reasons on the attendance screen, so employees can pick the break reason when checking out. To keep checking out at the end of the day free of friction, leave the reason optional.
  4. Optionally, in the Break Rounding setting, enter the number of minutes each break should be rounded to (for example 5 or 15). Leave it at 0 to keep the exact break duration (default).

Usage

To record a break:

  1. Check in as usual, from the attendance menu in the top bar or the kiosk.
  2. To start a break, check out and pick a break reason (for example Lunch Break).
  3. To end the break, check in again. The break is closed and the attendance of the day is reopened, instead of a new attendance being created.
  4. Repeat as many times as needed during the day, and check out without a break reason at the end of the day.

A break that is not ended on the same day (the employee went home after starting a break) is discarded: the attendance keeps the check out time of the break start, and the next check in starts a new attendance.

To review breaks:

  • Open an attendance record to see its breaks and the resulting Break Hours, Unpaid Break Hours and Net Worked Hours.
  • Go to Attendances > Reporting > Breaks for a list and pivot view of all breaks, grouped by employee, day or break type, with paid / unpaid filters.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • hr_attendance_rest_time_included (Tecnativa, up to 18.0) records breaks with the same check out / check in flow, but in a model of its own and always as paid time. Once it is migrated to 19.0, it could store its breaks in hr.attendance.break, using a paid break type, so both modules share the same data.
  • Starting a break directly from the kiosk without picking a reason, through a default break reason, could reduce the number of taps further.
