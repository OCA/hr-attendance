HR Attendance Report

This module provides an enhanced attendance reporting tool that allows HR managers to generate detailed reports filtered by employee, department, month, and year, with export capabilities in Excel and PDF formats.

Usage

  1. Go to Attendances > Reporting.
  2. Use filters for employee(s), department(s), month and year.
  3. Click "Export" to download the attendance report in PDF or Excel format.

Features

  • Filter by month, year, department, and employees.
  • Export reports to Excel or PDF.
  • Display total hours, absences, and working days.
  • Designed for HR audits and payroll integration.
